# AcmeCorp Product FAQ

Answers to the most common questions about our flagship products.

## Widget Pro

### What is Widget Pro?

Widget Pro is AcmeCorp's flagship smart home hub. It connects and controls up to 200 smart devices across Zigbee, Z-Wave, Matter, and Wi-Fi.

### Is Widget Pro waterproof?

**No.** Widget Pro is designed for **indoor use only** and is rated IP20. It is not waterproof or water-resistant. Exposure to moisture will void the warranty.

### What is the warranty on Widget Pro?

Widget Pro comes with a **2-year manufacturer warranty** covering defects in materials and workmanship. Extended warranties (up to 5 years) can be purchased at checkout.

### Does Widget Pro work without the internet?

Yes, most features work locally over your home network. However, remote access, voice assistants (Alexa, Google), and cloud automations require an internet connection.

### How much does Widget Pro cost?

- **Widget Pro (Base)**: $199
- **Widget Pro Plus** (with battery backup): $249
- **Widget Pro Max** (enterprise, 1000+ devices): $499

## Widget Lite

### What is Widget Lite?

Widget Lite is a budget-friendly smart plug. Compact, easy to install, compatible with Alexa and Google Assistant.

### How many Widget Lite units can I use?

Up to 50 Widget Lite plugs can be paired with a single Widget Pro hub. They can also be used standalone via Wi-Fi.

### Does Widget Lite support schedules?

Yes, schedules can be configured via the AcmeCorp mobile app (iOS 15+ and Android 10+).

## General Questions

Yes. All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256). AcmeCorp is SOC 2 Type II certified and GDPR compliant.

We never sell customer data to third parties.
