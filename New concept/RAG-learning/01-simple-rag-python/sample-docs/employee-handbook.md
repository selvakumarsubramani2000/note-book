# AcmeCorp Employee Handbook (Excerpt)

**Version**: 5.1
**Last Updated**: February 2026

## Working Hours

Standard working hours at AcmeCorp are **Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:30 PM** local time, with a 30-minute lunch break.

Employees may work flexible hours with manager approval, as long as core hours (10:30 AM – 3:30 PM) are respected.

## Remote Work Policy

All employees are entitled to **up to 3 days of remote work per week**. Remote days must be coordinated with the team and approved by the direct manager.

Fully remote roles are available on a case-by-case basis for senior positions (Level 5 and above).

## Paid Time Off (PTO)

| Tenure | Vacation Days/Year | Sick Days/Year |
|--------|--------------------|-----------------|
| 0–2 years | 15 | 10 |
| 3–5 years | 20 | 10 |
| 6–10 years | 25 | 12 |
| 10+ years | 30 | 15 |

Employees also receive:
- **12 paid public holidays** per year.
- **5 personal days** (use-it-or-lose-it).
- **Paid parental leave**: 16 weeks (primary caregiver), 6 weeks (secondary).

## PTO Request Process

1. Submit PTO request via the HR portal at least **2 weeks in advance**.
2. Your manager has 3 business days to approve or decline.
3. Emergency PTO may be approved retroactively.

## Health & Wellness Benefits

- Medical, dental, and vision insurance (AcmeCorp covers 80% of premiums).
- $500/year wellness stipend (gym, therapy, yoga, etc.).
- Employee Assistance Program (EAP) — 24/7 counseling.
- Free annual health check-up.

## Professional Development

- **$2,000/year learning budget** (books, courses, conferences).
- 3 paid days per year for conferences/training.
- Free access to LinkedIn Learning and O'Reilly.

## Code of Conduct Highlights

- Treat colleagues with respect. Harassment is a terminable offense.
- Protect confidential company and customer data.
- Disclose conflicts of interest to HR.
- Report ethical concerns to `ethics@acmecorp.com` (anonymous option available).

## Termination & Resignation

- **Resignation**: Provide at least **2 weeks notice** in writing.
- **Termination for cause**: Immediate, with final pay within 72 hours.
- **Layoffs**: 60-day notice or pay-in-lieu, per local law.
