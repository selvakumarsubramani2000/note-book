# AcmeCorp Refund Policy

**Version**: 2.3
**Last Updated**: March 2026
**Owner**: Finance Department

## Overview

AcmeCorp offers a **30-day money-back guarantee** on all products purchased directly from acmecorp.com or authorized resellers.

## Eligibility

To be eligible for a refund:
- The product must be returned within **30 calendar days** of purchase.
- The product must be in **original condition** with all accessories and packaging.
- You must provide the original order number or receipt.
- Digital downloads are refundable only if unopened / unused.

## Refund Timelines

| Payment Method | Processing Time |
|----------------|-----------------|
| Credit / Debit Card | 5–7 business days |
| PayPal | 2–3 business days |
| Bank Transfer | 14 business days |
| Store Credit | Immediate |

Refunds are processed within **14 business days** of receiving the returned item at our warehouse.

## High-Value Purchases

Purchases over **$500** require **manager approval** before a refund can be issued. The Finance Manager or Regional Director must sign off on the refund request.

Purchases over **$5,000** additionally require **CFO approval**.

## Non-Refundable Items

The following items are **NOT eligible** for refunds:
- Custom-made products
- Gift cards
- Subscription services after 14 days of use
- Opened software licenses (digital)
- Clearance / final sale items

## How to Request a Refund

1. Email `refunds@acmecorp.com` with your order number.
2. Our team responds within 1 business day with a Return Authorization (RA) number.
3. Ship the item back using the prepaid label.
4. Once received and inspected, your refund is initiated.

## Disputes

If you are unhappy with a refund decision, you can escalate to `appeals@acmecorp.com`. Disputes are reviewed by a senior manager within 5 business days.
